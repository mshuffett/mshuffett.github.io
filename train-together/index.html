Partner rhythm: On heavy lifts, person A does a set → person B does a set → each rests until roughly 2 minutes have passed since their previous set. For small accessories, alternate or superset different muscle groups and rest 60–90 seconds after the pair. Keep 1–3 reps in reserve and stop for pain.
